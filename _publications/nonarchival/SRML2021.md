Workshop paper presented at the ICML 2021 Workshop on Socially Responsible Machine Learning. It formalizes direct discrimination as a direct causal effect of protected attributes on decisions and induced discrimination as a change in the influence of associated non-protected features, and proposes post-processing methods that nullify the protected attribute's influence while preserving the influence of the remaining features. The extended version appeared at ACM FAccT 2022 as [Marrying Fairness and Explainability in Supervised Learning](/publication/facct2022).
